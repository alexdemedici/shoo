Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version

Shoo cleans your iPhone on your iPhone. Your photos, videos and contacts never leave your device. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Photos and videos

To find similar photos, duplicates, screenshots and large videos, Shoo reads your photo library on your device using Apple's Photos and Vision frameworks. Nothing is uploaded, copied to a server or shared. Items you choose to delete are moved to "Recently Deleted" in the Photos app by iOS, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Contacts

If you use the duplicate contacts feature, Shoo reads your contacts on your device to find duplicates and, only after you confirm, merges them. Contact data is never uploaded or shared.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. To check whether you have unlocked Shoo Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipts. RevenueCat does not receive your photos, contacts, name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy for details.

Data stored on your device

Shoo stores a few settings locally, such as how many free cleanups you have used. Deleting the app removes them.

Analytics and advertising

Shoo contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Shoo does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on this page with a new date.
