Common questions

Where do deleted photos go?
Into "Recently Deleted" in the Photos app. You can restore them there for 30 days. Empty that album to get the space back right away.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Scrammy analyzes your library on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Scrammy, tap the gear icon, then "Restore purchases".

How do I cancel the yearly plan?
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Scrammy › Cancel Subscription.
